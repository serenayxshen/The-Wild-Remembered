The Wild Remembers

Smoke is young, hungry, and responsible for more than himself. Lead your six-wolf pack across a living wilderness of forest and river valley. Hunt, rest, recover, and decide who to trust.

Every animal moves through its own life. Prey flee and learn. Wolves remember rescue, failure, shared food, and ignored warnings. Trust changes the way your pack answers you.
